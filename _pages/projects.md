## GPN

Genomic language models for variant effect prediction and genetic studies. My contributions include model development and benchmarking, including GPN-Star.

[Code →](https://github.com/songlab-cal/gpn) · [GPN-Star paper →](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-11005-5)

---

## CPT-1

Cross-protein transfer learning for missense variant effect prediction. I co-developed the method, combining protein-model predictions with deep mutational scanning data.

[Code →](https://github.com/songlab-cal/CPT) · [Paper →](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13059-023-03024-6)

---

## DECENT

An R package for differential expression analysis of single-cell RNA-seq data. I developed the beta-binomial capture model and software implementation.

[Code →](https://github.com/cz-ye/DECENT) · [Paper →](https://doi.org/10.1093/bioinformatics/btz453) · [Analysis scripts →](https://github.com/cz-ye/DECENT-analysis)

---

## BCTiLs.net

An R Shiny application for exploring tumor-infiltrating T-cell data from breast cancer.

[Researcher resources →](https://www.tilsinbreastcancer.org/researcher-resources/) · [Paper →](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41591-018-0078-7)
