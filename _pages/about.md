I’m a postdoctoral fellow at the [Bakar Computational Biomedicine Initiative](https://bcbi.berkeley.edu/) (BCBI). I develop machine learning and statistical approaches to study large genomic datasets and understand human disease and complex traits.

My research explores how deep learning models of DNA sequence can reveal the effects of genetic variation. Recently, I have worked on genomic language models that use evolutionary data for self-supervised learning, capturing functional constraints and useful representations of the genome.

At BCBI, I’m interested in connecting molecular, cellular, and clinical data with collaborators across disciplines.

## Research interests

- Genomic language models and sequence modeling
- Statistical methods for genetics and genomics
- Integrating data across biological scales
